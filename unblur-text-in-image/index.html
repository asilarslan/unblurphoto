Try the Text mode

Select Text in the tool above. It sharpens letter edges and stretches contrast so faint print stands out against the paper. Start around 50 and increase slowly; text turns blotchy when pushed too far.

  • Crop first. Cropping to the paragraph you need gives the tool more pixels per letter.
  • For camera-shake streaks on a page, try Motion blur mode instead.
  • Pixelated screenshots (blocky, not soft) need AI upscaling. Use the app.

When the AI app helps

Unblur Image's AI handles soft photos of documents, screenshots that look pixelated and whiteboard shots at full resolution. Keep the strength conservative: on text, an aggressive AI can “invent” a letter that looks right but isn't. Always double-check numbers, names and amounts against another source before relying on them.

What can't be done

No tool can honestly recover text that was deliberately blurred or pixelated to hide it, such as a redacted name or a censored licence plate. That information is gone, and anything an AI “reveals” is a guess. Unblur Image is meant for your own photos that came out soft by accident.

Shoot documents sharper next time

  1. Use the Notes app's Scan Documents feature. It flattens the page and boosts contrast.
  2. Hold the phone parallel to the page and tap the text to focus.
  3. Avoid zooming in digitally; move closer instead.
  4. Use window light, not overhead lamps that cast your phone's shadow.

FAQ

Can I unblur a censored or pixelated name?

No, and no honest tool can. Deliberate blurring removes the information; any ‘recovered’ text is made up.

What resolution do I need for readable text?

As a rule of thumb, letters should be at least 15–20 pixels tall after cropping. Below that, sharpening alone rarely helps.
